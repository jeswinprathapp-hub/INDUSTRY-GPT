Industrial Machine A Operations Manual
DEMO / SYNTHETIC DATA — NOT FOR REAL INDUSTRIAL USE

1. General Information
Machine Name: Industrial Machine A
Model: IMA-2000

2. Operating Limits
The recommended operating temperature is 60°C–80°C.
Maximum Pressure: 150 PSI.

3. Startup Procedure
- Ensure power supply is connected.
- Press the green START button.
- Wait for 5 minutes for the system to reach 60°C.

4. Shutdown Procedure
- Press the red STOP button.
- Disconnect power supply.
- Wait for the system to cool down below 40°C before maintenance.

5. Emergency Shutdown Procedure
In case of overheating (above 80°C) or fire:
- Press the EMERGENCY STOP button immediately.
- Evacuate the area.
- Contact the safety supervisor.

6. Maintenance Schedule
Preventive maintenance must be performed every 3 months.
Lubricate the main gear.
Check for any pressure leaks.

7. Troubleshooting
If the machine overheats:
- Check the cooling fan.
- Ensure the air vents are not blocked.
- Stop the machine and wait for it to cool down.
